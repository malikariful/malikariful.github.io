I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at The University of Alabama, with expected graduation in May 2027. My research focuses on peer code review in research software, empirical software engineering, and the use of large language models and AI-powered agents in software engineering. Before my doctoral studies, I served as a Lecturer and later Assistant Professor at Bangladesh University of Business and Technology.

Research in Progress

AI-Powered Code Review Agents in Research Software

I am investigating the types of coding issues identified by AI-powered code review agents in research software pull requests, the extent to which developers act on AI-generated review comments, and the characteristics associated with developer action. A planned extension will examine factors developers report as influencing their decisions through interviews or surveys with original pull-request authors.
